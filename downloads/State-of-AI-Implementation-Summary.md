# The State of AI Implementation — Executive Summary

> The annual report on what actually closes the gap between AI spend and AI value.
> Built from platform and assessment data. We publish the failure data, then beat it.

**Honesty note.** Every headline figure in the full report is treated as a standard to prove,
not a slogan to assert — reported with method, baseline, and audit trail. This summary is ungated;
the full report (methodology, data tables, sector breakdowns) is available by request.

## Five findings

1. **The gap is real — and it is never the model.** The distance between AI investment and value
   realized persists because of unready data, undefined success, un-redesigned workflows, and absent
   governance. All four are fixable.

2. **Data readiness leads.** Organizations that pass a hard data-readiness gate before building
   outperform those that rush to a demo on unready data. The floor decides the ceiling.

3. **Agentic AI is the divider.** Leaders have moved past commoditized generative AI into governed
   agentic systems — the durable, hard-to-copy advantage. Generative is the ante, not the edge.

4. **Governance raises the ceiling.** Mature CORE governance correlates with deploying AI at higher
   stakes. Governance is the unlock, not the brake.

5. **Compounding compounds.** Teams that design each win to feed the next pull steadily ahead of those
   paying the reset tax on disconnected pilots. The compounding is the product.
